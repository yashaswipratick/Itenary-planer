THE DESTINATION

Coorg, in brief

Kodagu (also called Coorg) is a hilly district in southwestern Karnataka on the eastern slopes of the Western Ghats. It is known for coffee and pepper plantations, forested hills and the Kaveri River’s source at Talakaveri. Madikeri is the district headquarters.
